Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fundito ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign up or sign in using Google or Facebook, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address

2.2 Financial Data You Provide

Fundito is an expense tracking app. The financial data you enter is stored securely:

  • Expense amounts and categories
  • Wallet names and balances
  • Currency preferences
  • Notes and descriptions you add to transactions

2.3 Device and Usage Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage patterns (e.g., features used)
  • Crash logs and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Fundito app
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Send you scheduled notifications and reminders (with your permission)
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Respond to support requests

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides enterprise-grade security with encryption at rest and in transit. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Third-Party Services

Fundito uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Authentication and database hosting
  • Google Sign-In — Authentication
  • Facebook Login — Authentication
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and purchase management

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only:

  • With service providers who assist in operating our app (as listed above)
  • If required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights or the safety of our users

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your financial data (CSV/PDF)
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

9. Children's Privacy

Fundito is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of Fundito after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

Email: support@fundito.app
